FAQ

Questions about call centre & support.

Which channels can you support?+
We handle inbound and outbound voice, email, live chat and multichannel support, as well as helpdesk and ticket management. Tell us the channels you need and we will design the team around them.
Can you handle both inbound and outbound calls?+
Yes. We run inbound customer service as well as outbound programmes such as telephone sales, lead generation, appointment scheduling, follow-ups and customer retention.
Do we get a dedicated team?+
Yes. Our managed teams include dedicated agents, team leaders and quality specialists who work on your account.
Can we start small and grow?+
Yes. Many clients begin with a focused pilot and scale as volumes and confidence grow. See our engagement models on the Managed BPO Teams page.
How will we know how the team is performing?+
Through agreed KPIs and SLAs, call monitoring and QA scorecards, and regular performance reports.
