From First Call to Fully Managed

A structured five-stage model that gets your operation live quickly and keeps it improving.

01

Discover

We map your customer journeys, volumes, channels, languages and performance goals.

  • Operations assessment
  • Volume & channel analysis
  • Success criteria agreed
02

Design

We define scope, staffing model, SLAs, KPIs and escalation workflows.

  • Service level agreements
  • Staffing & shift plan
  • Escalation matrix
03

Deploy

We recruit, train and onboard your dedicated team on your systems, products and brand voice.

  • Screening & language assessment
  • Product & systems training

Manage

  • QA scorecards
  • Workforce scheduling
05

Improve

Regular reporting and business reviews drive continuous improvement.

  • KPI & SLA reporting
  • Coaching & training plans
  • Quarterly business reviews
