FAQ

Questions about CRM & quality.

Can you work inside our existing CRM?+
Yes. We administer and work within your CRM and case management tools under controlled access.
What does case management include?+
Ticket and case logging, prioritisation, tracking, escalation, resolution and reporting, using defined workflows.
How is quality measured?+
Through call monitoring, interaction assessments and QA scorecards, alongside KPI and SLA monitoring agreed with you.
Will we see the results?+
Yes. Quality assurance and performance reports are shared with you on an agreed schedule.
Do you recommend training?+
Yes. QA findings turn into training recommendations and coaching plans for continuous improvement.
