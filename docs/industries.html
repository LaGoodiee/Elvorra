FAQ

Questions about industry support.

Do you support airlines and travel companies?+
Yes. This is a priority area for us. We support passenger enquiries, booking and reservation assistance, ticketing enquiries, complaints handling and flight disruption communication across email, chat and telephone.
Can you provide multilingual passenger support?+
Our advertised languages are English, French, German and Spanish. Multilingual support is subject to verified staffing availability, and we confirm language coverage against assessed personnel before agreeing a service.
Will your team work in our booking or CRM systems?+
Yes. Our teams work in your systems under controlled access and follow your documented procedures.
Can we start with a pilot?+
Yes. We offer structured paid pilots starting with 20–30 personnel, then expand following successful delivery.
