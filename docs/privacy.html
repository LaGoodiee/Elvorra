Privacy Policy

How ELVORRA OPERATIONS handles information submitted through this website.

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

ELVORRA OPERATIONS is an international business process outsourcing and managed operations company based in Abuja, Nigeria. You can contact us at .

Information we collect

When you submit our enquiry form we collect the information you provide:

  • Contact details: full name, job title, company name, business email, country and contact number.
  • Enquiry details: services required, team size, preferred start date, languages, coverage, working hours, time zone and any additional project information.
  • Technical information needed to protect the form from spam, such as the time taken to complete it.

We do not ask for sensitive personal information. Please do not include confidential customer data or personal data about third parties in your message.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to your enquiry, prepare proposals, arrange consultations, send you a confirmation and manage a potential business relationship. We do not sell your information.

Legal basis

We process your information based on your consent when you submit the form, and our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries, in line with applicable data protection laws, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and, where it applies to you, the GDPR or UK GDPR.

Where information is stored and who sees it

Submitted enquiries are delivered to our business email inbox and recorded in a spreadsheet, both held in Google Workspace services. Access is limited to authorised ELVORRA OPERATIONS personnel. Google acts as a service provider and may process data in countries outside your own. This website is hosted by a third-party hosting provider.

Third-party content

To display this website we load fonts from Google Fonts and photographs from Unsplash. Your browser may share your IP address with these providers when loading them. We do not currently use advertising or analytics cookies. Your light or dark display preference is stored in your own browser only.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry records for as long as needed to respond to you and manage any resulting relationship, and then delete or anonymise them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your information, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent at any time. To exercise these rights, email us at .

Security

Form submissions are sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. We limit access to enquiry records and take reasonable steps to protect them, but no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
