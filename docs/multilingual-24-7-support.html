FAQ

Questions about multilingual & 24/7 support.

Which languages do you support?+
Our advertised languages are English, French, German and Spanish. We confirm language availability against assessed personnel before agreeing each engagement and do not promise coverage we cannot verify. Other languages are considered on request.
Can you cover nights, weekends and holidays?+
Yes. We build shift-based teams and schedules around the hours your customers need support, including round-the-clock coverage.
How do you keep quality consistent across shifts?+
Each shift has team leaders and quality monitoring, and every team works to the same scripts, knowledge base and KPIs.
What happens when there is an urgent issue overnight?+
We agree escalation paths with you in advance, so urgent cases reach the right person quickly at any hour.
Can we combine languages in one team?+
Yes. We can build teams that cover several languages and route customers to the right agents.
