FAQ

Questions about pilots, teams & partnerships.

How large is the paid pilot?+
ELVORRA OPERATIONS offers structured paid pilot engagements starting with 20–30 personnel, depending on your operational requirements.
What happens after the pilot?+
The pilot includes agreed KPIs and SLAs and a structured transition into longer-term BPO contracts. Following successful delivery, you can expand into larger outsourcing arrangements.
What is a managed BPO team?+
A team of dedicated personnel, team leaders, QA specialists and operational management that we mobilise, supervise and report on for your business.
Are you a recruitment agency?+
No. ELVORRA OPERATIONS is a managed operations partner. We do not simply supply manpower: we manage the people, processes, quality and service delivery.
How quickly can a team be mobilised?+
We mobilise teams according to your requirements, role mix, language needs, staffing availability and an agreed onboarding timeline.
Do you partner with other BPO companies?+
Yes. We welcome subcontracting, overflow support and long-term delivery partnerships with established BPO companies.
