FAQ

Questions about managed teams & partnerships.

What is a managed BPO team?+
A team of dedicated agents, team leaders, QA specialists and operational management that we recruit, deploy, supervise and report on for your business.
Are you a recruitment agency?+
No. ELVORRA OPERATIONS is a managed operations partner. Recruitment coordination and workforce deployment exist to build and run your managed operation.
Can we start with a pilot?+
Yes. A pilot programme lets you validate quality and fit before scaling into a dedicated team or managed service.
Do you partner with other BPO providers?+
Yes. We offer overflow, multilingual and after-hours capacity to established providers through subcontracting partnerships.
How do we begin a partnership?+
Start with an introductory call, then we scope the work, prepare a tailored proposal and agree a pilot or go-live plan.
